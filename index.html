About The Project

Understanding Urban
Economic Dynamics

City Dynamics: GDP & Productivity Insights is a comprehensive data analytics project built as a final-year engineering capstone. It leverages interactive Power BI dashboards to explore how cities generate wealth, sustain growth, and drive productivity across diverse economic sectors.

The project analyzes real-world datasets spanning multiple years and cities, offering stakeholders a visual and intuitive way to understand complex economic indicators, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven policy recommendations.

Key Insights

What the Data Reveals

Critical findings distilled from our comprehensive analysis of urban economic indicators and productivity benchmarks.

1
GDP Growth Analysis

The Top 10 Cities by GDP reveal a significant concentration of economic output. Metro cities consistently dominate the GDP rankings, with the gap between top and bottom performers widening over the 2019–2026 period.

2
Employment vs Unemployment

Employment and Unemployment analysis across the 18 cities shows an inverse relationship. Cities with diversified economies across IT, Manufacturing, and Tourism exhibit lower unemployment rates and more stable job markets.

3
Population vs GDP

The scatter plot analysis reveals that higher population does not always translate to higher GDP. Several smaller cities achieve higher GDP per capita, indicating better productivity and resource utilization.

4
Sector Contribution

The treemap visualization shows IT and Manufacturing as the dominant sectors across the 18 cities, while Tourism and Agriculture vary significantly by region. Per capita income differences across states highlight regional economic disparities.

5
Tourism & Income Inequality

Tourism contribution analysis shows coastal and heritage cities benefiting the most. Income inequality data reveals that cities with heavy reliance on a single sector tend to have wider income gaps between high and low earners.

6
Policy Impact & Regime Analysis

GDP comparison across Pre-Independence, Post-Independence, and Post-LPG eras shows dramatic economic acceleration after liberalization in 1991. GDP across ruling regimes reveals varying growth trajectories tied to key policy decisions.

Future Scope

Where We Go from Here

Planned enhancements and research directions to expand the project's analytical capabilities and real-world impact.

AI-Powered Predictions

Integrate machine learning models to forecast GDP growth and productivity trends for the next 5–10 years based on historical data patterns.

Real-Time Data Feeds

Connect live data APIs for real-time economic indicators, enabling stakeholders to monitor city performance as events unfold.

Mobile Application

Develop a dedicated mobile app with push notifications for critical economic alerts and on-the-go dashboard access.

Expanded City Coverage

Scale the analysis beyond 18 cities to cover all state capitals and major tier-2 and tier-3 cities across India for deeper regional insights.
